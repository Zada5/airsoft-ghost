About

Airsoft Ghost is an Israeli airsoft YouTube channel. Every week it posts head-cam POV games filmed at airsoft fields in Israel — ghillie-suit sniper stalks, close-quarters (CQB) fights and night-vision games — plus short clips on Instagram and Facebook.

I'm the bush that shoots back. Full-camo ghillie sniper gameplay, patient stalks, sneaky flanks and long-range BB hits — filmed POV, edited tight. Israel is the channel's number-one audience country, with viewers from all over the world.

Where can I play airsoft in Israel?

At licensed fields such as Magen Airsoft in central Israel and WW3 Airsoft Club in Kfar Sumei. Since 2025, replicas may only be held through licensed clubs. Details in our airsoft in Israel guide.

Play safe

Airsoft is a sport played at organized fields, with full-seal eye protection and agreed safety rules. Airsoft replicas are not toys and are regulated in many countries — know and follow your local laws, and never carry or display a replica in public.
