1. Who we are

This policy covers (a) this website and (b) "Airsoft Ghost Uploader", a private software tool used to manage the Airsoft Ghost YouTube channel. Both are operated by the independent creator behind the Airsoft Ghost YouTube channel, based in Israel ("Airsoft Ghost", "we", "us"). Contact: dira85783 [at] gmail.com.

2. Information collected on this website

We collect no personal information directly. The site uses no cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, forms, accounts or embedded third-party content.

Hosting: the site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). To deliver and secure the service, GitHub may automatically process technical data such as your IP address and request details, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs to identify visitors.

3. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and message only to reply and to handle your request. We keep it only as long as needed for that purpose (or as required by law) and then delete it. We do not share it, except where required by law.

4. YouTube and other linked sites

Links to YouTube, GitHub and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies. When you watch, comment on or subscribe to the Airsoft Ghost channel, that data is processed by YouTube/Google under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. As channel owner we can only see what YouTube makes available to creators, such as public comments, public usernames and aggregated, anonymized analytics.

5. Airsoft Ghost Uploader — use of Google user data

Who uses it: only the channel operator, signing in with the operator's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other person's Google account data.

What it accesses and why — via Google OAuth 2.0, the YouTube Data API v3 and the YouTube Analytics API, with these scopes:

ScopeWhat it allows
youtubeManage the operator's own channel: video titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and comment replies.
youtube.uploadUpload videos to the operator's own channel.
youtube.readonlyRead the operator's own channel and video information.
yt-analytics.readonlyView the operator's own channel analytics reports.
yt-analytics-monetary.readonlyView the operator's own channel revenue reports.

How it is used: only to upload, schedule and manage videos, playlists and comment replies on the Airsoft Ghost channel, and to read that channel's own analytics to improve its content.

Storage and security: OAuth tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, protected by its operating-system account. They are not stored on any server, and are sent only to Google, over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing: Google user data is not sold, rented or traded; is not shared with third parties except service providers processing it on the operator's behalf to perform the functions above; is not used for advertising; is not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending; and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Airsoft Ghost Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Airsoft Ghost Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion: authorization tokens are kept until access is revoked. Data retrieved from the APIs is kept only as long as needed to manage the channel. If access is revoked, data stored through that authorization is deleted within 7 days.

Revoking access: at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
